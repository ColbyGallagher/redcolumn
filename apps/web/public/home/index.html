The PDF tools AEC work depends on

All the basics are here, done properly.

  • Markup and annotation

    Clouds, callouts, highlights, stamps, text, dimensions and more, with a markups list you can sort, filter and export.

  • Scaled measurement

    Calibrate a scale, then measure length, area, perimeter, volume, angle and count straight from the drawing.

  • Sheets and pages

    Sets, page labels, bookmarks, split, extract, insert, crop and batch operations on whole drawing sets.

  • Compare and overlay

    Compare revisions and overlay one drawing on another to see what changed.

  • Sign and secure

    Digital signatures, redaction, flattening and PDF/A output.

  • Works with your files

    Opens and saves standard PDFs, and keeps markups intact when files go to and from other PDF tools.
