# The Markups list

The Markups list, at the bottom of the screen, shows **every markup and measurement** in the document in a table. Use it to review, track and report on your markups.

![The Markups list](img/list-overview.png)

## Open it

It is open by default. If you can't see it, choose {{Window > Bottom Panel}} and click the **Markups** tab. Drag the top edge of the panel up to make it taller.

## Work with rows

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Go to a markup on the drawing | Click its row. The page jumps to it and selects it. |
| Select several | [[Ctrl]]+click or [[Shift]]+click rows. |
| Add or change a comment | Click in the **Comments** cell and type. |
| Set a status | Pick it in the **Status** cell. See [Statuses](#list-status). |
| See replies | Replies show under the comment, with who wrote them. |
| More actions | Right-click a row (the same menu as on the drawing). |

The bottom of the list shows totals, for example **Area ×2: 1,128.13 sf**.

## The list's toolbar

![The Markups list toolbar](img/list-bar.png)

| # | Control | What it does | Read more |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Search markups** | Finds markups by any text in them. | [Search, filter and sort](#list-filter) |
| 2 | **Hide All** | Hides every markup on the page (they stay in the list). | |
| 3 | **Filter** | Shows a filter box above each column. | [Search, filter and sort](#list-filter) |
| 4 | **All markups** | Picks a saved filter. | [Search, filter and sort](#list-filter) |
| 5 | **Filter Builder…** | Builds a filter from conditions. | [Search, filter and sort](#list-filter) |
| 6 | **On page** | Shows, dims or hides markups the filter leaves out. | |
| 7 | **Group** | Groups rows by a column, with subtotals. | [Group and total](#list-group) |
| 8 | **Columns ▾** | Shows or hides columns. | [Columns](#list-columns) |
| 9 | **Status ▾** | How the status column shows history. | [Statuses](#list-status) |
| 10 | **Manage Columns…** | Your own columns and statuses. | [Columns](#list-columns) |
| 11 | **Export…** | CSV or PDF report. | [Export the list](#list-export) |
| 12 | **Sync…** | Keeps the list in a Google Sheet or Excel workbook. | [Keep the list in a spreadsheet](#sheet-sync) |

> **Tip:** The **Links** tab next to **Markups** lists the sheet links in the document. See [Sheet links](#links).
