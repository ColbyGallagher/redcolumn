# A tour of the screen

Learn the names of the areas on the screen. The rest of this help uses these names.

![The redcolumn screen with a drawing open](img/workspace-tour.png)

| # | Area | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Menu bar** | Every command, sorted into File, Edit, View, Document, Batch, Tools, Window and Help. |
| 2 | **Toolbar** | Your most-used markup tools, plus colour, line width and other settings for the tool you are using. |
| 3 | **Tabs** | One tab per open document. Click a tab to switch documents. |
| 4 | **Panel rail** | Buttons that open the side panels (Thumbnails, Properties, Measurements and more). |
| 5 | **Side panel** | The panel you picked on the rail. |
| 6 | **Drawing area** | The page itself. You draw, measure and select markups here. |
| 7 | **Navigation bar** | Zoom, page number, page size and the page's **Scale**. |
| 8 | **Bottom panel** | The **Markups** list (every markup in the document) and **Links**. |
| 9 | **Status bar** | Snap and grid switches on the left, the pointer position on the right. |

## Show or hide areas

- To hide or show the toolbar, side panel or bottom panel, use the {{Window}} menu.
- To make the panel rail narrower, click **Collapse** at the top of it.
- To give a panel more room, drag the edge between the panel and the drawing.

> **Tip:** Most things also have a right-click menu. If you are not sure what you can do with something, right-click it.
