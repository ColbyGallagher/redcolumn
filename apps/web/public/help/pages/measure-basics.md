# Measuring: start here

redcolumn measures straight from the drawing, using the drawing's **scale**. Get the scale right first, and every measurement after it is right.

## The three steps

1. **[Set the scale](#scale)** for the page. Pick it from the list, type it, or [calibrate](#calibrate) from a known length.
2. **Pick a measure tool** from {{Tools > Measure}}.

   ![The Tools, Measure menu](img/menu-measure.png)
3. **Click on the drawing.** The measurement appears on the page, in the [Markups list](#markups-list) and in the [Measurements panel](#measurements-panel).

## Which tool do I need?

| To measure | Use | How |
|---|---|---|
| A straight distance | [Length](#measure-length) ([[M]]) | Click two points. |
| A distance along a path | [Polylength](#measure-polylength) ([[Alt+Shift+Q]]) | Click points, press [[Enter]]. |
| A floor or wall area | [Area](#measure-area) ([[Alt+Shift+A]]) | Click corners, click the first one to close. |
| The distance around a shape | [Perimeter](#measure-perimeter) ([[Alt+Shift+P]]) | Click corners, press [[Enter]]. |
| How many of something | [Count](#measure-count) ([[Alt+Shift+C]]) | Click each one, press [[Enter]]. |
| An angle | [Angle](#measure-angle) ([[Alt+Shift+G]]) | Click a point, the corner, another point. |
| A circle | [Diameter](#measure-diameter-radius) ([[Alt+Shift+D]]) or [Radius](#measure-diameter-radius) ([[Alt+Shift+U]]) | Click two points. |
| A curve | [Arc Length](#measure-arc-length) | Click start, middle, end. |
| Concrete, fill, excavation | [Volume](#measure-volume) ([[Alt+Shift+V]]) | Outline the area, then set its depth. |
| A whole room in one click | [Smart Fill](#smart-fill) ([[J]]) | Click inside the room. |
| Every copy of a symbol | [Symbol Search](#symbol-search) | Drag a box around one. |

> **Tip:** Measure tools aren't on the toolbar at first. To add the ones you use, right-click the toolbar › **Toolbar Tools** › **Measure**. See [Customise the toolbar](#toolbar).

> **Warning:** Measurements are only as good as the scale and the drawing. Check a known dimension before you rely on a takeoff for pricing or ordering.
