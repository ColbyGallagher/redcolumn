# Flatten and unflatten markups

**Flattening** burns markups into the page, so every PDF reader shows them exactly and nobody can edit them. Use it before you issue a final copy.

## Flatten the whole document

1. Choose {{Document > Flatten…}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+M]]).

   ![The Flatten window](img/flatten.png)
2. Leave **Allow markup recovery** ticked so you can bring the markups back later with Unflatten.
3. Click **OK**.

Links stay clickable and form fields stay fillable.

## Flatten only some markups

Select them, then right-click › **Flatten**.

## Unflatten

Choose {{Document > Unflatten}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+U]]). Markups flattened with recovery on come back as normal markups.

> **Tip:** To keep your working file editable, [Publish](#publish) a flattened copy instead of flattening the original.
