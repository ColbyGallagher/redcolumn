# Arrange the panels

## Show or hide whole areas

Use the {{Window}} menu:

![The Window menu](img/menu-window.png)

- **Tools Toolbar** — the toolbar at the top.
- **Left Panel** — the panel rail and side panel.
- **Bottom Panel** — the Markups list and Links.

## Choose which panels are on the rail

Right-click the panel rail and tick or untick panels:

![The panel rail's right-click menu](img/rail-menu.png)

**Reset Panel Order** puts them back in their usual order.

## Reorder the rail

Drag a panel's button up or down the rail.

## Dock panels on the right

Drag a panel's rail button, or the title at the top of the open panel, to the right-hand edge of the window. A second rail appears there; drop the panel on it. Panels on the right open in their own side panel, so you can have one panel open on each side at once — for example Thumbnails on the left and File Access on the right.

Drag a panel back to the left rail to move it back. You can also right-click a panel's button and choose **Dock … on the Right** (or **on the Left**), or **Dock All Panels on the Left** to put everything back. **Window › Right Panel** shows or hides the right side panel.

## Resize

- Drag the edge between the side panel and the drawing to make the panel wider or narrower.
- Drag the top edge of the bottom panel up or down.
- Click **Collapse** at the top of the rail to show only icons.

> **Tip:** Panel choices, including which side each panel is docked on, are saved in your [profile](#profiles).
