# Dark pages, dimmer and line weights

These change how pages look **on screen only**. Printing and saving are not affected.

## Dark Mode (Pages)

Shows white pages as dark, with light lines. Easier on the eyes at night.

1. Choose {{View > Dark Mode (Pages)}}.

   ![A drawing shown in dark mode](img/dark-pages.png)
2. Choose it again to turn it off.

## Dimmer

Makes bright white pages a little darker, without reversing the colours.

Choose {{View > Dimmer}} (or press [[Ctrl+F5]]). Choose it again to turn it off.

## Disable Line Weights

Draws every line in the PDF as a thin line. This makes busy drawings easier to read when zoomed out, and makes it easier to click on the exact line you want.

Choose {{View > Disable Line Weights}}. Choose it again to turn it off.

## Always Show Reply Indicators

Shows a small speech bubble with a count on markups that have [replies](#comments).

Choose {{View > Always Show Reply Indicators}}.
