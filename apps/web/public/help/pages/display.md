# Dark pages, dimmer and line weights

These change how pages look. Saving is not affected. Printing is affected only by [Disable Line Weights](#disable-line-weights).

## Dark Mode (Pages)

Shows white pages as dark, with light lines. Easier on the eyes at night.

1. Choose {{View > Dark Mode (Pages)}}.

   ![A drawing shown in dark mode](img/dark-pages.png)
2. Choose it again to turn it off.

## Dimmer

Makes bright white pages a little darker, without reversing the colours.

Choose {{View > Dimmer}} (or press [[Ctrl+F5]]). Choose it again to turn it off.

## Disable Line Weights

Draws every line in the PDF as a thin, solid line at any zoom, without its thickness or dash pattern. This makes busy drawings easier to read, and makes it easier to see the exact point you are measuring from. Markups keep their own line weights and styles, and so do filled shapes: a thick line that the PDF stores as a filled shape stays thick.

While it is on, the drawing also **prints** without line weights. Turn it off before printing to print line weights as drawn. The file itself is never changed.

Choose {{View > Disable Line Weights}}. Choose it again to turn it off.

## Always Show Reply Indicators

Shows a small speech bubble with a count on markups that have [replies](#comments).

Choose {{View > Always Show Reply Indicators}}.

## Keep View Between Pages

When you change page with the next and previous arrows, the page keys, First and Last Page, the page box or the thumbnails, the new page opens at the same zoom and on the same part of the sheet, so you can flip between floors or revisions and stay on the same detail. It is on by default.

- If the new page is a different size and you were viewing the whole page (or its width), it is fitted the same way. If you were zoomed in, the same fraction of the sheet is shown, with the zoom adjusted so the same part of the drawing fills the view.
- In continuous layout the page opens at the part of the sheet that was in the middle of the view.
- Side-by-side layouts always fit the spread.
- Bookmarks, links and search results still go to their own destination.

Choose {{View > Keep View Between Pages}}, or **Preferences > Display**, to turn it off; each page then opens as before.
