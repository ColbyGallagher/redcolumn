# Arc

Draws a curved line through three points.

## Draw an arc

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Lines & Shapes > Arc}}, or press [[Shift+C]].

   ![Tools menu with Arc highlighted](img/pick-arc.png)
2. Click where the arc **starts**.
3. Click a point the curve passes **through** (usually near the middle).
4. Click where the arc **ends**. The arc is finished.

   ![An arc drawn through three points](img/draw-arc.png)

## Change the arc

Select it (press [[V]] and click it), then drag any of its three handles to reshape the curve.

> **Note:** To measure the length of a curve, use [Arc Length](#measure-arc-length).
