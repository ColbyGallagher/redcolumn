# Live Sessions

A **Live Session** lets several people mark up the same drawings at the same time, from anywhere. Everyone sees each other's markups appear as they are made, can chat, and can send markup alerts. Every action is kept in the session's **Record**.

> **Note:** Live Sessions are **experimental** and may change.

## How it works

- The **host** starts the session in **their own** Google Drive or OneDrive. The documents and markups live in a folder there. There is no redcolumn server.
- **Attendees** join from an invite link or email, and sign in with their Google or Microsoft account.
- The host decides who can view, who can comment, and who can edit everyone's markups.

![The Sessions panel](img/sessions-panel.png)

## Open the Sessions panel

Click **Sessions** on the panel rail. **My Sessions** lists sessions you have hosted or joined. **Active** shows current ones; **All** includes ended and removed ones.

## In a session

Once you are in a session, the panel shows:

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | The session's PDFs. Click one to open it. Session tabs show a **Live** badge. |
| **Attendees** | Who is in the session and who is online now. |
| **Chat** | Type in **Message everyone** and click **Send**. |
| **Record** | Everything that happened: who joined, markups added and changed, statuses, messages. **Report** makes a PDF Session Report; **CSV** downloads the Record. |
| **Leave session** | Leaves and closes its documents. You can rejoin later. |

## Markup alerts

Right-click a markup › **Send Markup Alert…** and type a message. Everyone in the session is shown that markup with your message.

## Offline

If your connection drops, keep working. The panel says **Offline**, and your markups sync when the connection returns.
