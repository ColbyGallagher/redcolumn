# Stamp

Places a stamp such as **APPROVED**, **REVIEWED** or **FOR CONSTRUCTION**. Stamps fill in your name and the date by themselves.

## Place a stamp

1. Press [[S]] for the last stamp you used, or choose {{Tools > Stamp}} and click the stamp you want.

   ![The Stamp menu](img/menu-stamp.png)
2. Click on the page to place it at its normal size, **or** drag a box to make it bigger or smaller.

   ![An APPROVED stamp on the plan](img/draw-stamp.png)

The **Stamp** button on the toolbar places the stamp you used last.

   ![The Stamp button on the toolbar](img/pick-stamp.png)

## Built-in stamps

Approved · Approved as Noted · Reviewed · Revise and Resubmit · Rejected · For Construction · Not for Construction · Preliminary · Draft · Void · Confidential

Stamps with a second line show **By** *your name* **on** *today's date*. Your name comes from the **Author** box in the Edit menu (see [Preferences](#preferences)).

> **Tip:** Want your own wording or company stamp? See [Make your own stamps](#stamps-manage). To stamp many files at once, see [Batch](#batch).
