# Passwords and permissions

Save a protected copy of a PDF: one that needs a password to open, or that limits printing, copying or editing.

1. Choose {{Document > Security…}} (or press [[Ctrl+L]]).

   ![The Security window](img/security.png)
2. To require a password to open the file, type it under **Password to open**, and again under **Again**.
3. To limit what people can do, tick **Limit what can be done**, choose what is allowed, and set a permissions password.
4. Click **Save Protected Copy…** and choose where to save it.

The copy is encrypted (AES-256). **The document on this device is not changed.**

> **Warning:** Keep the password safe. Nobody, including redcolumn, can open a PDF if its open password is lost.

## Open a protected PDF

When you open a PDF with a password, redcolumn asks for it. After that, every tool works on it as normal. To keep the protection, save with **Security…** again.
