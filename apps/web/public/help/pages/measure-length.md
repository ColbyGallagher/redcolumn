# Length

Measures a straight distance between two points.

1. [Set the scale](#scale) for the page.
2. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Length}} (or press [[M]]).

   ![Tools, Measure, Length](img/pick-length.png)
3. Click the start point, then the end point. (You can also press, drag and let go.)

   ![A length measured across the living room](img/measure-length.png)

The length is written on the line, and listed in the [Markups list](#markups-list) and [Measurements panel](#measurements-panel).

> **Tip:** Hold [[Shift]] to keep the line horizontal, vertical or at 45°. Hold [[Alt]] to stop it snapping.

## Change how it looks

On the toolbar, **Arrows** sets the ends of the line (arrows, ticks or none). In the [Properties panel](#properties) you can set an **Offset** to draw it like a dimension line, away from what it measures.

## Move or turn the text

Select the length. A yellow knob sits on its text, with a round knob above it.

- **Drag the yellow knob** away from the line to move the dimension line out with it; the extension lines stretch back to the measured points. Drag it along the line to slide the text; past an arrow, the line runs on out to the text. Hold [[Shift]] to slide it without moving the line.
- **Drag the round knob** to turn the text. Hold [[Shift]] to turn it in 15° steps.

The Properties panel shows these as **Offset**, **Text slide** and **Text turn**. The same works for a **Dimension** markup.
