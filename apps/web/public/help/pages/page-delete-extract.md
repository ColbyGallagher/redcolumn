# Delete, extract and replace pages

## Delete pages

1. In the [Thumbnails panel](#thumbnails), select the pages to delete.
2. Click **Delete**, then **OK**.

Or, to delete the page you are on, choose {{Document > Delete Pages…}} (or [[Ctrl+Shift+D]]) and click **OK**.

> **Warning:** Markups on deleted pages are deleted too. Press [[Ctrl+Z]] straight away if you deleted the wrong page.

## Extract pages to a new PDF

1. Choose {{Document > Extract Pages…}} (or [[Ctrl+Shift+X]]).

   ![The Extract Pages window](img/extract-pages.png)
2. Choose the **Pages**: the current page, all pages, or a list like `2-4, 7`.
3. Optional:
   - **One file per page** — each page becomes its own PDF (downloaded together as a zip).
   - **Delete the pages from this document afterwards** — moves the pages out instead of copying them.
4. Click **OK**. The new PDF is saved to your computer.

## Replace pages with pages from another PDF

Use this to swap in a new revision of a sheet while keeping your markups.

1. Choose {{Document > Replace Pages…}} (or [[Ctrl+Shift+Y]]).

   ![The Replace Pages window](img/replace-pages.png)
2. Choose which **Pages** to replace.
3. Under **New pages**, click **Choose File** and pick the PDF with the new pages.
4. Click **OK**.

The pages are replaced in order. **Markups stay on the replaced pages.**

> **Tip:** For a whole new revision of a set, [Slip sheet](#slip-sheet) does this sheet by sheet, matching sheet numbers.
