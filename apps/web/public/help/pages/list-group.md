# Group and total

Group the list to see markups and measurements bunched by subject, page, status, Space, author or any column, with a count for each group.

1. On the list's toolbar, pick a column in **Group**, for example **Subject**.

   ![The list grouped by subject](img/list-grouped.png)
2. Click a group's heading to fold it up or open it.
3. Pick **None** to stop grouping.

## Totals

The line at the bottom of the list adds up the measurements that are showing, for example **Area ×2: 1,128.13 sf · Length ×1: 29'-6"**. Filter or search the list and the totals follow.

> **Tip:** For a takeoff by room, add [Spaces](#spaces) and group by **Space**.
