# Slip sheet a new revision

When new revisions of some sheets arrive, **Slip Sheet** puts them into your set in place of the old ones. Each sheet keeps its place and **its markups**, and the old set is kept as a [revision](#revisions).

1. Open the set you have been marking up.
2. Choose {{Document > Slip Sheet…}}.

   ![The Slip Sheet window](img/slip-sheet.png)
3. Under **New revisions**, click **Add files…** and pick the PDFs with the new sheets (or **Add from the library…**).
4. Choose how to **Match sheets**:
   - **By sheet number** — A-101 replaces A-101, wherever it is. Best for drawing sets.
   - **By page order** — the new pages replace pages 1, 2, 3…
5. Leave these ticked unless you have a reason not to:
   - **Add new sheets that don't match any page, at the end.**
   - **Move markups to line up with the new sheets** — fixes markups when the new sheet was plotted in a slightly different place.
6. Click **Slip Sheet**.

> **Tip:** Afterwards, [Compare](#compare) the old revision with the new one to see what changed under your markups.

To slip sheet many sets, use {{Batch > Slip Sheet…}}.
