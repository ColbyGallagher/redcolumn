# Thumbnails panel

The Thumbnails panel shows a small picture of every page. Use it to move around, reorder pages, and change pages in bulk.

## Open it

Click **Thumbnails** on the panel rail.

![The Thumbnails panel](img/thumbnails-panel.png)

Under each picture is the page's **label** (sheet number) and page number, for example *A-101 · 1*.

## The controls

![The controls at the top of the Thumbnails panel](img/thumbnails-controls.png)

1. **Rotate anticlockwise** the selected pages.
2. **Rotate clockwise** the selected pages.
3. **Delete** the selected pages.
4. **Extract** the selected pages to a new PDF.
5. **Insert PDF…** — insert another PDF's pages after the selection.
6. **Scale** — set the scale of the selected pages. See [Set the scale](#scale).
7. **Label** — the sheet number of the selected page. See [Page labels](#page-labels).
8. **Thumbnail size** — drag to make the pictures bigger or smaller.

## Select pages

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Select one page (and go to it) | Click it. |
| Add pages one at a time | Hold [[Ctrl]] and click. |
| Select a run of pages | Click the first, hold [[Shift]] and click the last. |

![Two pages selected](img/thumbnails-selected.png)

## Reorder pages

Drag a page (or a selection of pages) up or down and drop it where it should go.

## The Document menu

Every page command is also in the {{Document}} menu:

![The Document menu](img/menu-document.png)

> **Note:** Page changes (rotate, delete, insert, reorder) change the PDF. They can be undone with [[Ctrl+Z]]. Markups move with their pages.
