# Share or email

Choose {{File > Share / Email…}} (or press [[Ctrl+E]]).

![File menu: Publish, Share / Email and Print](img/menu-file-share.png)

- **On a phone or tablet** (and some computers), your device's share sheet opens. Pick the app to send the PDF with (Mail, Messages, Teams, WhatsApp…).
- **Otherwise**, redcolumn saves a copy with your markups to your Downloads folder and opens a new email. Attach the saved file to the email.

The copy includes your markups. To send a flattened, cleaned copy, [Publish](#publish) first and send that file.

> **Tip:** To review a document together in real time instead of emailing copies, start a [Live Session](#sessions).
