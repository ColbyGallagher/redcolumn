# Line

Draws a straight line.

## Draw a line

1. Click **Line** on the toolbar, or press [[L]].

   ![The Line button on the toolbar](img/pick-line.png)
2. Press where the line starts, drag to where it ends, and let go.

   ![A line drawn across the living room](img/draw-line.png)

> **Tip:** Hold [[Shift]] while you drag to keep the line exactly horizontal, vertical or at 45°.

## Change the line

- **Move an end:** select the line (press [[V]], click it), then drag the square at either end.
- **Colour, thickness, dashes:** use the toolbar. See [Change colour, line and fill](#style).
- **Add arrowheads:** in the [Properties panel](#properties), under **Line endings**, set **Start** or **End**. Or use the [Arrow](#tool-arrow) tool.
- **Exact length:** turn on [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale) and type the length as you draw.

> **Note:** A Line is just a drawing. To measure a distance, use [Length](#measure-length).
