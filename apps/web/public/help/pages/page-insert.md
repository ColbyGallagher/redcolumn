# Insert pages

## Insert pages from another PDF (or a picture)

1. Go to the page that the new pages should come **after**.
2. Choose {{Document > Insert > Pages from PDF…}}.

   ![Document, Insert](img/menu-document-insert.png)
3. Pick the PDF (or a photo, such as a JPG or PNG) and click **Open**.

All its pages are inserted after the current page.

![A new page inserted after page 1](img/inserted-page.png)

> **Tip:** In the [Thumbnails panel](#thumbnails), select a page and click **Insert PDF…** to insert after that page. Or drag the pages into place afterwards.

## Insert blank pages

1. Choose {{Document > Insert > Blank Pages…}}.

   ![The Insert Blank Pages window](img/insert-blank.png)
2. Choose the **Page size** (for example the same as page 1, or Letter, A4, ARCH D…).
3. Type how many **Pages**.
4. Choose where to **Insert** them (before or after a page).
5. Click **Insert**.
