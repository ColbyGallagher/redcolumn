# Rectangle

Draws a box.

## Draw a rectangle

1. Click **Rectangle** on the toolbar, or press [[R]].

   ![The Rectangle button on the toolbar](img/pick-rect.png)
2. Press at one corner, drag to the opposite corner, and let go.

   ![A rectangle drawn around bedroom 2](img/draw-rect.png)

> **Tip:** Hold [[Shift]] while you drag to draw a perfect square.

## Fill it in

Tick **Fill** on the toolbar to fill the rectangle with colour. Lower the **opacity** so the drawing shows through. See [Change colour, line and fill](#style).

## Change it

- **Resize:** select it, then drag a corner or side handle.
- **Rotate:** select it, then drag the round handle above it. Hold [[Shift]] to turn in 15° steps.
- **Exact size:** turn on [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale); the width and height appear on the toolbar.
