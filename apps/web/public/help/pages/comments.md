# Comments, replies and status

Every markup can carry a comment, a conversation of replies, and a status, so a review can be tracked to the end.

## Add a comment

1. Right-click the markup and choose **Add Comment…**.
2. Type the comment.

   ![Typing a comment on a cloud](img/comment-dialog.png)
3. Click **OK** (or press [[Ctrl+Enter]]).

You can also type straight into the **Comments** column of the [Markups list](#markups-list), or the **Comment** box in the [Properties panel](#properties).

## Reply to a comment

1. Right-click the markup and choose **Reply**.
2. Type your reply and click **OK**.

   ![Typing a reply](img/reply-dialog.png)

Replies are shown under the markup in the Markups list, with who wrote them and when.

## Set a status

1. Right-click the markup and point at **Set Status**.

   ![The Set Status menu](img/markup-menu-status.png)
2. Click **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Completed** or **Cancelled** (or **None**).

The status, who set it and when, is recorded in the **Status** column of the list:

![The Markups list showing status and comment](img/list-with-reply.png)

> **Tip:** You can make your own statuses (for example *Open*, *Closed*, *For Info*). See [Statuses](#list-status).

## Check and Flag

- **Check** (right-click › **Check**) ticks a markup as reviewed. It is separate from the status.
- **Flag** (right-click › **Flag**) marks it so it shows in the [Flags panel](#flags-panel).

## Show reply bubbles on the page

Choose {{View > Always Show Reply Indicators}} to show a small bubble with a count on markups that have replies.
