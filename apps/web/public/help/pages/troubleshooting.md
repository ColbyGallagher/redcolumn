# Something is not working

Try these in order.

## 1. Undo

If something just changed that you didn't want, press [[Ctrl+Z]]. See [Undo History](#undo) to go back several steps.

## 2. Press Esc

A tool may still be picked, or a box half-drawn. Press [[Esc]] (twice) to go back to the Select tool.

## 3. Check the messages

Messages appear at the bottom of the screen. A red message explains what went wrong.

## 4. Reload

Close the tab (or the app) and open it again. Your documents and markups are kept.

## 5. Update

Choose {{Help > Check for Updates}}. If a new version is ready, it loads when you are not in the middle of something.

## 6. Problems with one PDF

- **It opens slowly or looks wrong:** try {{Document > Repair PDF}}.
- **It is very large:** try {{Document > Reduce File Size…}}.
- **It has a password:** you will be asked for it when it opens.

## 7. Running out of space

Your browser limits how much it stores. In {{File > Preferences…}} › **Advanced** › **Offline** you can see how much is used, and click **Keep on This Device** so the browser doesn't clear your documents. Remove files you no longer need from [File Access](#file-access).

## Still stuck?

[Get help and send feedback](#get-help). Include the log file: it helps work out what went wrong.
