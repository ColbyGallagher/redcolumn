# Arc Length

Measures the length along a curve.

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Arc Length}}.

   ![Tools, Measure, Arc Length](img/pick-arcLength.png)
2. Click where the curve **starts**.
3. Click a point **on** the curve, around the middle.
4. Click where the curve **ends**.

   ![An arc length measurement](img/measure-arcLength.png)

Drag the handles afterwards to fit the curve more closely.
