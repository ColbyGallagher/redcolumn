# Offset

Makes a parallel copy of a line, polyline or shape at a set distance. Handy for wall thicknesses, kerbs and setbacks.

1. Select **one** line, polyline, polygon or rectangle.
2. Choose {{Edit > Offset}} (or press [[O]]).
3. Move the pointer to the side you want the copy. A dashed preview follows the pointer.

   ![The dashed offset preview beside a polyline](img/offset-preview.png)
4. Click to place the copy.

The new copy is selected, so you can offset again from it.

> **Tip:** For an exact distance, turn on [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale) and type the distance.
