# Make a PDF from the camera

Take photos of paper drawings, forms or site conditions with your device's camera, and make a PDF from them.

1. Choose {{File > Create > From Camera…}}.
2. If the browser asks to use the camera, click **Allow**.

   ![The Create PDF from Camera window](img/camera.png)
3. Point the camera at the page and click **Take Photo**. Take as many photos as you need; each becomes a page.
4. Click **Create PDF**.

The PDF opens in a new tab.

> **Tip:** Run [OCR](#ocr) on the new PDF to make its words searchable.

> **Note:** If the camera is blocked, allow it in your browser's site settings (the icon at the left of the address bar).
