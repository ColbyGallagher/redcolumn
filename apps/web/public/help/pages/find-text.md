# Find text and the Search panel

Find words in the document, in every open document, in your recent files or in a folder: in the drawings' text, markups, form fields, file names and file properties.

1. Press [[Ctrl+F]] (or choose {{Edit > Find Text}}). The **Search** panel opens.
2. Type what you are looking for and press [[Enter]].

   ![Search results in the Search panel](img/search-panel.png)
3. The results are listed by page (and by file, when you search more than one), with the words around each match. Click a result to go to it. The match is highlighted on the page.

   ![A search match highlighted on the drawing](img/search-hit.png)

Use **Next** to step through the matches.

## Where to search

Choose where to look from the list under the search box:

- **Current Document** — the document in front.
- **Current Page** — only the page in front.
- **All Open Documents** — every open tab.
- **Recents** — the files in {{File > Open Recent}}, open or not.
- **Folder** — the PDFs in a folder on your computer. Click **Choose…** to pick the folder. Click a result to open that file.

## Options

- **Include Subfolders** — with **Folder**, also searches the folders inside it.
- **Search Pages** — the drawings' own text.
- **Search Filenames** — file names (when searching more than one file).
- **Search File Properties** — title, author, subject, keywords, creator and producer.
- **Search Form Fields** — what has been typed into form fields.
- **Search Markups** — your markups' text, comments and replies.
- **Case Sensitive** — finds *Wall* but not *WALL*.
- **Whole Words Only** — finds *A-201* but not *A-2011*.

## Redact what you found

Click **Mark for redaction** to mark every match on the front document's pages for [redaction](#tool-redaction). Useful for removing a name or number throughout a document.

> **Note:** Scanned drawings have no text to search until you run [OCR](#ocr).
