# Find text and the Search panel

Find words anywhere in the document: in the drawing's text, and in markups and form fields.

1. Press [[Ctrl+F]] (or choose {{Edit > Find Text}}). The **Search** panel opens.
2. Type what you are looking for and press [[Enter]].

   ![Search results in the Search panel](img/search-panel.png)
3. The results are listed by page, with the words around each match. Click a result to go to it. The match is highlighted on the page.

   ![A search match highlighted on the drawing](img/search-hit.png)

Use **Next** to step through the matches.

## Options

- **Whole words** — finds *A-201* but not *A-2011*.
- **Form fields and markups** — also looks in your markups' text and in form fields.

## Redact what you found

Click **Mark for redaction** to mark every match for [redaction](#tool-redaction). Useful for removing a name or number throughout a document.

> **Note:** Scanned drawings have no text to search until you run [OCR](#ocr).
