# Rotate pages

Rotating pages turns them **in the PDF**, so they stay turned when you save. (To turn only your view, see [Rotate the view](#rotate-view).)

## Rotate the page you are on

Choose {{Document > Rotate Pages…}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+R]]). The page turns a quarter turn clockwise. Do it again to keep turning.

## Rotate several pages

1. In the [Thumbnails panel](#thumbnails), select the pages.
2. Click the **rotate anticlockwise** or **rotate clockwise** button at the top of the panel.

![A page rotated in the Thumbnails panel](img/rotated-page.png)

You can also right-click an empty part of the page › **Rotate Page** › **Clockwise**, **Counterclockwise** or **180°**.

Markups turn with the page. Press [[Ctrl+Z]] to undo.
