# Perimeter

Measures the distance around a shape: skirting, fencing, kerbs.

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Perimeter}} (or press [[Alt+Shift+P]]).

   ![Tools, Measure, Perimeter](img/pick-perimeter.png)
2. Click each corner, going around the shape.
3. Press [[Enter]] or double-click to finish. To include the side back to the start, click the first corner again before you finish.

   ![The perimeter of bedroom 2](img/measure-perimeter.png)

> **Tip:** To draw a curved section as you go, hold [[Ctrl]], press where the next corner goes and drag away from it. The section bends through the pointer; release to set it, then let go of [[Ctrl]]. Further clicks are straight again, and [[Backspace]] removes the curved corner. The length and area follow the curve.
