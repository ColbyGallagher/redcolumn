# Open a PDF

There are four ways to open a PDF. Use whichever is easiest.

## Open from the start screen

1. Click **Open a PDF…** in the middle of the screen.

   ![The Open a PDF button on the start screen](img/start-screen.png)
2. Pick the PDF on your computer and click **Open**.

The drawing appears and its pages are listed in the **Thumbnails** panel.

## Open from the File menu

1. Click {{File > Open…}} (or press [[Ctrl+O]]).

   ![File menu with Open highlighted](img/file-open.png)
2. Pick one or more PDFs and click **Open**. Each one opens in its own [tab](#tabs).

## Drag and drop

Drag a PDF from your file manager (File Explorer or Finder) and drop it on the drawing area.

## Open a recent file

- Choose {{File > Open Recent}} and click the file, **or**
- Click **File Access** on the panel rail and click the file. See [File Access](#file-access).

> **Tip:** If you [install redcolumn as an app](#install), you can also open PDFs straight from your file manager: right-click the PDF, choose **Open with**, then **redcolumn**.

> **Note:** A PDF you open is kept on this device so it opens instantly next time, even offline. Your markups are kept with it automatically.

## Password-protected PDFs

If the PDF has a password, redcolumn asks for it. Type the password and press [[Enter]].
