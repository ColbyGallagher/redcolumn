# Rulers, grid and crosshair

These are drawing aids. They only show on screen and are never printed or saved.

![Rulers, a dotted grid and the crosshair over the plan](img/view-aids.png)

## Turn them on and off

Click the switches on the left of the status bar. A switch is **on** when it is highlighted.

![The Grid, Snap Grid, Rulers and Crosshair switches](img/status-view-aids.png)

1. **Grid** — a dotted grid over the page ([[Shift+F9]]).
2. **Snap Grid** — the points you draw jump to the nearest grid dot ([[Ctrl+Shift+F9]]).
3. **Rulers** — rulers along the top and left of the drawing ([[Ctrl+R]]).
4. **Crosshair** — long cross lines that follow the pointer, to line things up across the whole sheet.

They are also in the {{View}} menu.

## Rulers

The rulers show distances on the page. When the page has a [scale](#scale), the rulers show real-world units (for example feet). Otherwise they show inches or millimetres on the paper.

## Change the grid spacing

1. Choose {{File > Preferences…}} (or press [[Ctrl+K]]).
2. Click **General**, then the **Grid & Snap** tab.
3. Choose the **Units** and change the **Grid Spacing**. The spacing is measured on the paper, whatever the drawing's scale.
4. Click **OK**.

## Pointer position

The right of the status bar shows where the pointer is, measured from the top-left corner of the page, as **X** and **Y**. When the page has a scale, these are real-world distances.
