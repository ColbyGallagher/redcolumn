# Overlay pages

Overlay stacks two revisions on one page in colour. What is the same in both shows dark. What is only in the old revision shows in one colour (red), and what is only in the new revision in another (blue).

1. Choose {{Document > Overlay Pages…}}.

   ![The Overlay Pages window](img/overlay-dialog.png)
2. Choose the **Old revision** and **New revision**, and the **Pages**.
3. Pick the colour for each revision.
4. Leave **Align pages automatically** ticked.
5. Click **Overlay**.

A new PDF is made and opened, named *Overlay - new over old*:

![An overlay: the moved wall shows in red and blue](img/overlay-result.png)

> **Tip:** Overlay is quick to read for moved walls and grids. For a list of each change with clouds, use [Compare](#compare).
