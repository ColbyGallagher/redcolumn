# Sound

Records a spoken note with your microphone and places it on the page. Handy on site when typing is slow.

## Record a sound note

1. Choose {{Tools > Sound…}}.
2. Click **Record**. If the browser asks to use the microphone, click **Allow**.

   ![The Sound window](img/sound-dialog.png)
3. Speak your note, then click **Stop**.
4. Click **Place on Page**, then click on the page where the note goes.

The note appears as a [File Attachment](#tool-attachment) icon. Double-click it to play it back.

> **Note:** Recordings are kept short because they are saved inside the PDF.
