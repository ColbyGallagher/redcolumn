# Angle

Measures the angle between two lines.

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Angle}} (or press [[Alt+Shift+G]]).

   ![Tools, Measure, Angle](img/pick-angle.png)
2. Click a point on the **first** line.
3. Click the **corner** (where the two lines meet).
4. Click a point on the **second** line.

   ![An angle measurement](img/measure-angle.png)

The angle in degrees is shown at the corner.
