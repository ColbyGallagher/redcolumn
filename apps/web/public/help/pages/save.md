# Save your work

redcolumn keeps your markups automatically as you work, so you will not lose them if the browser closes. To get a PDF **file** with your markups in it, you need to **Save**.

## Know when you have unsaved changes

A star (**\***) after the name on the tab means the PDF file on your computer does not have your latest changes yet.

![A tab showing a star for unsaved changes](img/tab-unsaved.png)

## Save

1. Click {{File > Save}} (or press [[Ctrl+S]]).

   ![File menu with Save and Save As highlighted](img/file-save.png)
2. The first time, choose where to save the file and click **Save**.

After the first save, **Save** writes straight to the same file each time, and the star disappears.

> **Note:** Some browsers (such as Firefox and Safari) cannot write to a file directly. In those browsers, Save downloads a copy called *name (markups).pdf* to your Downloads folder.

## Save As (save a new copy)

1. Click {{File > Save As…}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+S]]).
2. Type a new name, pick a folder, and click **Save**.

From then on, the tab works with the new file.

## Save every open document

Click {{File > Save All}} (or press [[Shift+F2]]). Each open document is downloaded as a copy with its markups.

## Throw away your changes (Revert)

1. Click {{File > Revert}}.
2. Click **OK** to confirm.

Every change since you opened the document is undone. If you change your mind straight away, press [[Ctrl+Y]] (Redo) to bring the changes back.

> **Tip:** Want the PDF exactly as it was before you marked it up? Right-click the tab and choose **Save a Copy of the Original…**
