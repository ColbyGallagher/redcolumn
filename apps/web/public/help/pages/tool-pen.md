# Pen

Draws freehand, like a pen on paper. Good for sketches and quick marks, especially with a stylus.

## Draw with the pen

1. Click **Pen** on the toolbar, or press [[P]].

   ![The Pen button on the toolbar](img/pick-pen.png)
2. Hold the mouse button (or pen) down and draw. Let go to finish the stroke.

   ![A freehand pen stroke](img/draw-pen.png)

Each stroke is its own markup. Change the colour and thickness on the toolbar before or after you draw.

> **Tip:** To rub out part of a stroke, use the [Eraser](#tool-eraser).
