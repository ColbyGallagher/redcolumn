# Several scales on one page (viewports)

Many sheets have details drawn at a different scale from the main plan. A **viewport** is a box around one of those details with its own scale. Anything you measure inside the box uses the viewport's scale.

## Add a viewport

1. Click **Measurements** on the panel rail.
2. Under **Viewports**, click **+**.

   ![The Add viewport button in the Measurements panel](img/viewports-add.png)
3. Drag a box around the detail.
4. Set the viewport's scale in its row in the panel, **or** click **Calibrate** and draw a line of known length inside the box.

Repeat for each detail. The page's own scale is used everywhere outside the boxes.

## Change or remove a viewport

In the Measurements panel, change its name or scale in its row, or click **×** to delete the selected viewport. Measurements already drawn update to the new scale. The wastebasket deletes every viewport on the page, or in the document.
