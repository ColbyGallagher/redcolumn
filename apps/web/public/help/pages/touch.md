# Touch screens and pens

redcolumn works on tablets, phones and touch-screen laptops.

## Finger gestures

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Zoom | Pinch two fingers together or apart. |
| Pan | Drag with two fingers. |
| Right-click (open the menu) | Press and hold one finger still for half a second. |
| Use a tool | Tap or drag with one finger, just like the mouse. |

## Using a pen (stylus)

When you use a pen, redcolumn turns on **palm rejection**:

- The **pen** draws and selects.
- A **single finger** pans the page instead of drawing, so a hand resting on the screen does not leave marks.
- Two fingers still zoom and pan.

## Small screens

On a phone, the side panels open over the drawing. Tap **×** on a panel to close it, or tap the drawing.

> **Tip:** Install redcolumn on your tablet to use it full screen and offline on site. See [Install the app](#install).
