# Zoom in and out

## Quickest ways

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Zoom in or out on one spot | Roll the mouse wheel over that spot (in **Single** page view). In **Continuous** view, hold [[Ctrl]] while you roll. |
| Zoom in one step | Press [[Plus]] |
| Zoom out one step | Press [[-]] |
| See the whole page | Press [[Ctrl+9]] (Fit Page) |
| Fill the window's width | Press [[Ctrl+0]] (Fit Width) |
| Show at real paper size | Press [[Ctrl+8]] (Actual Size) |

On a trackpad, pinch with two fingers.

## The zoom controls

The zoom controls are at the left of the navigation bar, under the drawing.

![Zoom controls in the navigation bar](img/nav-zoom.png)

1. **−** zooms out.
2. **The zoom box** shows the zoom. Click it, type a number (for example `200`) and press [[Enter]].
3. **+** zooms in.
4. **Fit page** shows the whole page.
5. **Fit width** makes the page as wide as the window.

## Zoom from the View menu

The same commands are at the top of the {{View}} menu.

![The View menu zoom commands](img/view-menu-zoom.png)

## Zoom to an exact area (Zoom tool)

1. Choose {{View > Navigation Tools > Zoom}} (or press [[Z]]).

   ![View, Navigation Tools](img/view-nav-tools.png)
2. Drag a box around the area you want to see.

   ![Dragging a zoom box over the kitchen](img/zoom-box.png)
3. Let go. That area fills the window.

Click once (without dragging) to zoom in a step. Hold [[Alt]] and click to zoom out a step. Press [[V]] to go back to the Select tool.

## Dynamic Zoom

1. Choose {{View > Navigation Tools > Dynamic Zoom}} (or press [[Shift+Z]]).
2. Press and hold on the drawing, then drag **up** to zoom in or **down** to zoom out.

> **Tip:** You can change how far each wheel notch or button press zooms in [Preferences](#preferences). There is also a setting to reverse the wheel direction.
