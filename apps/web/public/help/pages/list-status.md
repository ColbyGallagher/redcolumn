# Statuses

A **status** tracks where each markup is in a review: for example *Accepted*, *Rejected*, *Completed*.

## Set a status

Pick it in the **Status** cell of the [Markups list](#markups-list):

![The Status cell in the list](img/list-status-cell.png)

Or right-click the markup › **Set Status**. See [Comments, replies and status](#comments).

The standard statuses are **None**, **Accepted**, **Rejected**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**.

## Status history

Every change of status is recorded with who made it and when. Click **Status ▾** on the list's toolbar to choose what the column shows:

![The Status menu](img/list-status-menu.png)

- **Show Status History** — every change, newest first.
- **Show Latest Status Only** — just the current status.
- **Show Who Changed It and When** — the name and time under each status.

## Make your own statuses

1. Click **Manage Columns…** on the list's toolbar.
2. Click the **Statuses** tab at the top right.
3. Add, rename, recolour or remove statuses.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** Click **Set as profile default** so new documents use your statuses too.
