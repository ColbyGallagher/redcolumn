# Flag

Puts a small red flag on the page to mark a place you want to come back to. All flags are listed in the [Flags panel](#flags-panel), so you can jump between them.

## Place a flag

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Other > Flag}}.

   ![Tools menu with Flag highlighted](img/pick-flag.png)
2. Click where the flag goes.

   ![A flag in the kitchen](img/draw-flag.png)

## Add a note to the flag

Double-click the flag and type a note. Click **OK**.

## Jump to your flags

Click **Flags** on the panel rail. Click a flag in the list to go to it. See [Flags panel](#flags-panel).
