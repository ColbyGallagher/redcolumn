# Set the scale

Every page has its own scale. Until it is set, the navigation bar shows **Scale Not Set** and measurements are in paper units.

The scale controls are at the right of the navigation bar:

![The scale controls](img/scale-nav.png)

1. **Scale list** — pick a standard scale, or choose **Custom…** to open the custom scale dialog.

## Pick a standard scale

1. Go to the page.
2. Click the **Scale** list and pick the scale printed on the drawing, for example `1/4" = 1'-0"` or `1:100`.

The list offers imperial (Architectural and Engineering) or metric scales, depending on **Measurement System** in [Preferences](#preferences) › **Tools** › **Measure**.

## Set a custom scale

1. Click the **Scale** list and choose **Custom…**.
2. Enter the scale as it is written on the drawing, for example `1/4" = 1'-0"`, `1" = 20'` or `1:75`.
3. Under **Pages**, choose **All** to use the scale on every page, or the current page or a range.
4. Click **Apply**.

![The scale is set](img/scale-set.png)

> **Tip:** Not sure the drawing is printed to scale (for example a PDF of a scan)? [Calibrate](#calibrate) instead. It works out the scale from a dimension you know.

## Other ways

- **Different scales in parts of one page** (a detail at a larger scale): see [Viewports](#viewports).
- **Many pages with different scales**: see [Apply a scale to many pages](#scale-bulk).
- **Precision** (decimal places or inch fractions), and a custom scale's units: set them in the [Measurements panel](#measurements-panel).
