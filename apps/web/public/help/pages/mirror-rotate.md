# Mirror, Rotate, Align and Stretch

Move and reshape markups by picked points, like the AutoCAD commands of the same names. Find them under {{Edit > Modify}}. Snapping applies to every point you pick.

Mirror, Rotate and Align work on the selection. If nothing is selected, the first click picks a markup (with its group).

## Mirror

1. Select the markups and choose {{Edit > Modify > Mirror}}.
2. Click two points on the mirror line.

**Keep original** makes a mirrored copy. Untick it to flip the originals over. Text and pictures stay readable.

## Rotate

1. Select the markups and choose {{Edit > Modify > Rotate}}.
2. To turn by an exact amount, type an **Angle** in degrees (counter-clockwise), then click the base point.
3. To turn by reference, leave the angle empty. Click the base point, then a point along the current direction, then a point along the new direction.

## Align to Points

Moves and turns the selection so two points on it land on two target points.

1. Select the markups and choose {{Edit > Modify > Align to Points}}.
2. Click the first source point, then where it goes.
3. Click the second source point, then where that goes.

Tick **Scale to fit** to also grow or shrink the markups so both pairs line up exactly.

## Stretch

Moves the points inside a window and leaves the rest where they are, so shapes stretch.

1. Choose {{Edit > Modify > Stretch}}. It works on the selection, or on everything on the page when nothing is selected.
2. Click two corners of the crossing window.
3. Click a base point, then the point to move it to.

Markups wholly inside the window move. Rectangles stretch by their corners inside.
