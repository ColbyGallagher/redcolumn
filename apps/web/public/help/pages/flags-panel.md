# Flags panel

The Flags panel collects the things you want to come back to.

Click **Flags** on the panel rail.

![The Flags panel](img/flags-panel.png)

It lists:

| Section | What appears |
|---|---|
| **Flags** | [Flags](#tool-flag) you placed on pages. |
| **Flagged markups** | Markups you flagged (right-click › **Flag**). |
| **With a status** | Markups that have a [status](#list-status). |

Click any row to jump to it. **+ Add Flag** places a new flag.
