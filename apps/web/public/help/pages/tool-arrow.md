# Arrow

Draws a line with an arrowhead at the end, to point at something.

## Draw an arrow

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Lines & Shapes > Arrow}}, or press [[A]].

   ![Tools menu with Arrow highlighted](img/pick-arrow.png)
2. Press where the arrow **starts** (the tail), drag to the thing you are pointing at, and let go. The arrowhead goes where you let go.

   ![An arrow pointing at the kitchen](img/draw-arrow.png)

> **Tip:** Hold [[Shift]] while you drag to keep the arrow straight or at 45°.

## Change the arrowheads

1. Select the arrow (press [[V]] and click it).
2. In the [Properties panel](#properties), under **Line endings**, choose a different **Start** or **End** (filled, open, closed, tick and more). Set both for a double-headed arrow. **Ending size** makes the arrowheads bigger or smaller.

> **Tip:** Want the arrow to have text on it? Use a [Callout](#tool-callout) instead.
