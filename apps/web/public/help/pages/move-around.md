# Move around the page

Moving the page around (without changing the zoom) is called **panning**.

## Pan with the mouse

Any of these work:

- **Hold the middle mouse button** (the wheel) and drag. This works with every tool.
- With the **Select** tool, press on an **empty** part of the page and drag.
- In **Continuous** view, roll the mouse wheel to scroll up and down through the pages.

## The Pan tool

If you prefer a hand tool:

1. Choose {{View > Navigation Tools > Pan}} (or press [[Shift+V]]).
2. Drag the page in any direction.
3. Press [[V]] when you are done, to go back to the Select tool.

## Go back to where you were

redcolumn remembers your previous views, like the Back button in a web browser.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Go back to the previous view | Press [[Alt+Left]], or click **↶** in the navigation bar. |
| Go forward again | Press [[Alt+Right]], or click **↷**. |

This is handy after you follow a link to another sheet.

> **Tip:** Want dragging on an empty part of the page to draw a selection box instead of panning? Turn on **Drag on the Page to Select Markups** in [Preferences](#preferences) › **Tools** › **Markup**. You can still pan with the middle mouse button.
