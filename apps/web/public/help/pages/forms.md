# Fill in a form

PDF forms have boxes to type in, tick boxes and lists. Fill them in right on the page.

## Fill in fields

1. With the Select tool, click in a field. Field boxes are tinted blue.
2. Type the value.

   ![Typing in a form field](img/fill-field.png)
3. Press [[Tab]] to go to the next field, or [[Enter]] to finish. [[Esc]] cancels what you typed.

Click a tick box to tick or untick it. Click a list to choose from it.

![A form with fields filled in](img/form-filled.png)

## The Forms panel

Click **Forms** on the panel rail (or {{Tools > Form > Forms Panel}}). Every field is listed in tab order, and you can fill them in from here too.

![The Forms panel](img/forms-panel.png)

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Reset** | Clears every field back to its default. |
| **Export XFDF** | Saves the values in a file most PDF programs can import. |
| **Export CSV** | Saves the values for a spreadsheet. |
| **Import…** | Fills the form from XFDF, CSV or JSON data. |

> **Tip:** Need to sign the form? See [Sign with your signature](#signatures).

[Save](#save) when you are done. The values are saved in the PDF's fields, so any PDF program can read them.
