# Cut a hole out of an area

Take out areas you don't want counted, like a stair void, a lift shaft or a courtyard.

1. Draw the [Area](#measure-area) (or Volume) as normal.
2. Select it and open the [Properties panel](#properties).
3. Click **Add cutout**.

   ![The Add cutout button](img/cutout-button.png)
4. Click around the hole, and click its first corner to close it.

   ![An area with a cutout](img/measure-cutout.png)

The area shown is now the outer area minus the hole. Add as many cutouts as you need.
