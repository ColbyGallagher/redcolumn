# Spaces (rooms and zones)

A **Space** is a named area such as a room, a unit or a floor. Markups inside a Space belong to it, so you can group and total your takeoff room by room.

## Add a Space

1. Click **Spaces** on the panel rail.
2. Click **+ Add Space**.

   ![The Spaces panel with no spaces yet](img/spaces-add.png)
3. Click around the room's outline and click the first corner to close it.
4. Type the room's name and press [[Enter]].

   ![The kitchen as a Space](img/space-drawn.png)

The Space appears in the panel with its area and how many markups are inside it.

![The Spaces panel listing the kitchen](img/spaces-panel.png)

> **Tip:** [Smart Fill](#smart-fill) with **Fill as: Space** makes a Space in one click.

## Use Spaces

- In the [Markups list](#markups-list), the **Space** column names the Space each markup is in. Group the list by Space to see totals per room.
- A Space inside another (a room inside a floor) is shown inside it in the panel.
- Untick **Show** to hide Spaces on the page. They still group markups.

## Rename or delete

Click the Space in the panel, then the ✎ (rename) or × (delete) button. Deleting a Space keeps the markups that were in it.
