# Draw to Scale (exact sizes)

Type exact lengths, angles, widths and heights while you draw, in real-world units from the page's [scale](#scale).

## Turn it on

Click **Draw to Scale** in the status bar (or {{Tools > Draw to Scale}}).

![The Draw to Scale switch in the status bar](img/status-reuse.png)

## Draw a line or polyline of an exact length

1. Pick the tool (Line, Polyline, Polygon, or a measure tool).
2. Click the start point.
3. Type the length, for example `12` or `12'6"`. A bar appears at the bottom of the drawing.

   ![The Draw to Scale bar](img/sketch-drawing.png)
4. Press [[Tab]] and type an **Angle** if you need one (0° is to the right, counter-clockwise). Leave it empty to go toward the pointer.
5. Press [[Enter]] (or click **Place**). The point is placed. Keep typing for the next side, or press [[Enter]] on an empty box to finish.

## Draw a box of an exact size

For a Rectangle, Ellipse or Cloud: click the first corner, type the **Width**, press [[Tab]], type the **Height**, press [[Enter]].

## Change the size of a drawn markup

Select a line, rectangle or polyline. With Draw to Scale on, its sizes show on the toolbar. Type new ones.

> **Note:** Units come from the page's scale. If the page has no scale, sizes are on the paper (inches or millimetres). [Set the scale](#scale) first.
