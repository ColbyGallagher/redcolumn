# Polyline

Draws a line with several straight sections, joined at corners.

## Draw a polyline

1. Click **Polyline** on the toolbar, or press [[Shift+N]].

   ![The Polyline button on the toolbar](img/pick-polyline.png)
2. Click where the line starts.
3. Click at each corner. A line follows the pointer to show the next section.

   ![A polyline being drawn, corner by corner](img/draw-polyline-progress.png)
4. To finish, press [[Enter]] or double-click the last point.

   ![The finished polyline](img/draw-polyline.png)

> **Tip:** Clicked a corner in the wrong place? Press [[Backspace]] to remove the last point and carry on.

## Change the corners

Select the polyline (press [[V]], click it). Then:

- **Move a corner:** drag its square handle.
- **Add a corner:** drag the small knob halfway along a section.
- **Remove a corner:** hold [[Ctrl]] and click its handle.

> **Note:** To measure along a path, use [Polylength](#measure-polylength).
