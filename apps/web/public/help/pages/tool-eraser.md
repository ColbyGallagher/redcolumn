# Eraser

Rubs out parts of [Pen](#tool-pen) and [Highlighter](#tool-highlighter) strokes. It never erases the drawing itself.

![Eraser: rub out part of a pen stroke](gif/tool-eraser.gif)

## Erase

1. Choose {{Tools > Eraser > Eraser}}, or press [[Shift+E]].

   ![The Eraser menu](img/menu-eraser.png)
2. Drag the circle over the part of the stroke to remove.

   ![Part of a pen stroke rubbed out](img/draw-eraser.png)

## Eraser size

Choose {{Tools > Eraser > Small}}, **Medium** or **Large**.

## Erase whole markups

Turn on {{Tools > Eraser > Annotation Eraser (Whole Markups)}}. Now the eraser deletes **any** markup it touches, in one go.

> **Tip:** Erased the wrong thing? Press [[Ctrl+Z]].
