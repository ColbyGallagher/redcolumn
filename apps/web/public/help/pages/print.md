# Print

1. Choose {{File > Print…}} (or press [[Ctrl+P]]).

   ![The Print window](img/print-dialog.png)
2. Choose what to **Print**:
   - **Document and markups** — the drawing with your markups.
   - **Document only** — the drawing without markups.
   - **Markups only** — just the markups, on blank paper.
3. Choose the **Pages**: all, the current page, or a list like `1-3, 7`.
4. Options:
   - **Reverse order** — prints the last page first.
   - **Tile large pages onto** *Letter/A4…* **paper** — prints a big sheet in pieces you can tape together, at full size.
5. Click **Print…**.
6. Your computer's print window opens. Choose the printer, paper size, copies, scaling and orientation there, and print.

> **Tip:** To print to scale, choose **Actual size** (or 100%) in the computer's print window, on paper the same size as the drawing.

To print many files, use [Batch Print](#batch-summary-print).
