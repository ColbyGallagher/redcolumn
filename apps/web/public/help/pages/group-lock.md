# Group, lock and hide

All of these are in the {{Edit}} menu and on the right-click menu.

## Group

Grouped markups are selected and moved together.

- **Group:** select two or more markups, then press [[Ctrl+G]].
- **Ungroup:** select the group, then press [[Ctrl+Shift+G]].

> **Note:** A [Cloud+](#tool-cloud-plus) is a cloud and a callout grouped together.

## Lock

A locked markup cannot be moved, resized or deleted by accident.

- Select it and choose {{Edit > Lock}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+L]]). Choose {{Edit > Unlock}} (or press [[Ctrl+Shift+L]] again) to change it again.

## Hide

Hidden markups are not shown or printed, but are kept in the document.

- **Hide:** select it and choose {{Edit > Hide}}.
- **Show again:** choose {{Edit > Show Hidden Markups}}.

> **Tip:** To hide whole sets of markups at once, put them on a [layer](#layers), or use **Hide All** in the [Markups list](#markups-list).
