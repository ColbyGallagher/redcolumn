# Export the list (CSV, summary)

1. Click **Export…** on the list's toolbar (or {{File > Markup Summary…}}).

   ![The Export Markup Summary window](img/export-dialog.png)
2. Choose the **Format**:
   - **PDF** — a formatted report to print or share.
   - **CSV** — for Excel and other spreadsheets.
3. For PDF, choose the **Layout**:
   - **Comments only** — just the table of markups.
   - **Original + summary** — the marked-up document, with the table added at the end.
4. Type a **Title** for the report.
5. Choose which **Markups**: **Only those shown in the list** (after your filter) or **All markups**.
6. Tick the **Columns** to include.
7. Click **Export PDF** (or **Export CSV**). The file is saved to your computer.

> **Tip:** For a quick CSV of everything, choose {{File > Export > Markups CSV}}.

See also [Markup Summary report](#summary) and, for many files at once, [Batch Summary](#batch-summary-print).
