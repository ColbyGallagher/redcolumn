# Legend

A table, drawn on the page, that lists the kinds of markups on that page: each one's look, its name, and how many there are (with measurement totals). It updates by itself as you add or remove markups.

## Add a legend

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Other > Legend}}.

   ![Tools menu with Legend highlighted](img/pick-legend.png)
2. Drag a box where the legend should go.

   ![A legend listing two clouds and a rectangle](img/draw-legend.png)

Markups of the same type, **subject** and colour share a row. To make the legend tidier, give your markups clear subjects (for example *Demolish*, *New wall*) in the [Properties panel](#properties) or with the [Tool Library](#tool-library).

Change the text size and colours on the toolbar or in the Properties panel.
