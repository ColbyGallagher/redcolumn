# Colour processing

Change the colours of the drawing itself, for example to make a colour drawing grey so your red markups stand out.

1. Choose {{Document > Colour Processing…}}.

   ![The Colour Processing window](img/colour-processing.png)
2. Choose how to convert it:
   - **Greyscale** — every colour becomes a shade of grey.
   - **Black (everything but white)** — every colour becomes black.
   - **Replace one colour** — pick a colour in the drawing and what to change it to.
3. Choose the **Pages**.
4. Click **OK**.

Lines, fills, text and pictures change. Your markups are not changed.

> **Tip:** The drawing as it was is kept under {{Document > Revisions…}}, so you can go back to it.
