# Open a 3D model (IFC)

redcolumn opens building and civil models saved as IFC files (`.ifc`). Each model opens in a tab of its own, next to your PDFs.

![A road model open in its own tab, with the 3D ribbon tab](img/model-open.png)

1. The model's tab. Click it to bring the model to the front; click a PDF's tab to go back to the PDF.
2. The **3D** ribbon tab. It appears while a model is in front.
3. The view cube. Click a face to look from that side, or the small round button below it for a corner (isometric) view.

## Open a model

1. Choose {{File > Open}} (or press [[Ctrl+O]]).
2. Pick an `.ifc` file. You can also drag the file onto the window.

The first time a model opens, redcolumn converts it so it can be drawn quickly; a large model takes a while, and the tab shows how far it has got. The converted copy is kept on this device, so the same file opens much faster next time.

> **Note:** Models stay on your device, like your PDFs. Nothing is uploaded.

## The 3D ribbon tab

![The 3D ribbon tab](img/model-ribbon.png)

| Group | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Navigate** | How the mouse moves the camera: **Orbit**, **First Person**, **Walk** or **Plan View**; **Perspective** or **Orthographic**; **Fit All** and **Fit Selection**. See [Move around a 3D model](#model-navigate). |
| **Views** | Look from the top, front, back, left, right or a corner. The **3D view** list opens the model's storey plans and elevations; **Back to 3D** leaves them. |
| **Section** | **Section Box** and **Clipping Plane** cut the model open. **Remove Sections** takes the cuts away. |
| **Select** | **Select Elements**, then **Isolate**, **Hide**, **Show All** and **Clear Selection**. |
| **Show** | **Ghost Hidden**, **Grid**, **Mini-Map**, the model **On Sheets**, and **Sync with Sheet**. |
| **Measure** | Length, area, angle and volume in the model. |

## Close a model

Click the **×** on its tab. The model's memory is freed straight away.

> **Tip:** Right-click a model's tab and choose **Open in Split View** to see it beside a PDF. See [3D models and sheets on the map](#model-sheets).
