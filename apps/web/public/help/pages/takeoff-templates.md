# Takeoff templates

A takeoff template is a measuring tool set up for one kind of takeoff: the tool (Length, Polylength, Area, Count...), its subject, layer, style, slope and depth, and an [assembly](#assemblies) with its inputs. Pick the template and draw: every measurement gets all of it, so the takeoff is priced as you measure.

## Save a template

1. Set up one measurement the way you want them all: its subject, layer, look, slope and depth, and its assembly and inputs in the [Measurements panel](#measurements-panel).
2. Right-click it and choose **Save as Template…** (or select it and click **Save as Template** on the **Takeoff** tab).
3. Name the template and click **Save**.

The template is kept in the document, and in this browser's library so you can use it in other documents too.

## Measure with a template

1. On the **Takeoff** tab, click **Templates ▾**.
2. Pick a template. Its tool starts, set up as the template says.
3. Draw. Each measurement gets the template's subject, layer, style, slope and depth, and its assembly with its inputs.

Templates from the library are marked *(library)*. Picking one copies it into this document, with its assembly and the items and product lists that prices from.

## Manage templates

Choose **Templates ▾ › Manage Templates…** or {{Tools > Takeoff > Takeoff Templates…}} to:

- rename or delete the document's templates (click **Save** to keep the changes);
- **Use** one, or one from the library;
- **Export** the document's templates, with the assemblies, items and product lists they use, as one estimating file, and **Import…** such a file into another document.

> **Tip:** To share templates company-wide, export them once and keep the file where everyone can reach it. Anyone can import it into their own document; templates with the same name are replaced.
