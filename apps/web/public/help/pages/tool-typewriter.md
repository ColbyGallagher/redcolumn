# Typewriter

Adds plain text with no box, as if typed straight onto the page. Good for filling in title blocks and forms.

## Type on the page

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Text & Notes > Typewriter}}, or press [[W]].

   ![Tools menu with Typewriter highlighted](img/pick-typewriter.png)
2. Click where the text should start.
3. Type. The text grows as you type.
4. Press [[Ctrl+Enter]] or click away to finish.

   ![Typewriter text on the plan](img/draw-typewriter.png)

Double-click it later to change the words.
