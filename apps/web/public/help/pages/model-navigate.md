# Move around a 3D model

Choose how the mouse moves the camera in the **Navigate** group of the **3D** ribbon tab.

| Mode | How it moves |
|---|---|
| **Orbit** | Drag to turn around the model, right-drag to slide, and scroll to zoom. This is where you start. |
| **First Person** | Drag to look around from where the camera stands. |
| **Walk** | Walk through the model at eye height. See below. |
| **Plan View** | Look straight down with no perspective. Drag to slide and scroll to zoom. |

**Fit All** brings the whole model into view. **Fit Selection** frames what you have selected.

**Perspective** draws distant things smaller, as your eye sees them. **Orthographic** keeps sizes true everywhere, like a drawing.

## Walk

1. Click **Walk**. The camera drops to eye height (1.7 m) on the surface at the middle of the view, looking level.
2. Keep the pointer over the model, then press [[W]] [[A]] [[S]] [[D]] or the arrow keys to walk. Hold [[Shift]] to run.
3. Drag to look around.

You climb steps and kerbs up to half a metre high, stop short of walls, and fall off edges. Choose another mode to stop walking.

## Mini-map

Click **Mini-Map** (in the **Show** group) for a small plan of the model in the corner. The red arrow shows where the camera is and which way it faces.

![The mini-map in the corner of the 3D view](img/model-minimap.png)

Click anywhere on the mini-map to move the camera over that place.

> **Tip:** Click **Grid** to show a ground grid, which helps you judge sizes and heights.
