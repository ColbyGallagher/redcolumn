# Keyboard shortcuts

See every shortcut, and change any of them.

## See or change shortcuts

1. Choose {{File > Keyboard Shortcuts…}} (also {{Help > Keyboard Shortcuts Guide}}).

   ![The Keyboard Shortcuts window](img/shortcuts-dialog.png)
2. Find the command: scroll, or type in **Search commands or keys**.
3. To **change** a shortcut, click the command's key box, then press the new keys (for example [[Ctrl+Shift+K]]).
4. To **add** another shortcut, click **+** (or **Add** if it has none) and press the keys.
5. To **remove** a shortcut, click **×** beside it.
6. Click **Done**.

If the keys were used by another command, they move to this one and redcolumn tells you which command lost them. [[Esc]], [[Enter]] and [[Tab]] can't be shortcuts.

## Share or reset

- **Export…** saves your shortcuts to a file. **Import…** loads them.
- **Reset All** puts every shortcut back to the standard ones.

Shortcuts are part of your [profile](#profiles).

For a printable list of the standard shortcuts, see the [Shortcut reference](#shortcut-list).
