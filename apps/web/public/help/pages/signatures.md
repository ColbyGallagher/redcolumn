# Sign with your signature

Create your signature (and initials) once, keep it on this device, and place it on any document.

## Create your signature

1. Click **Signatures** on the panel rail.

   ![The Signatures panel](img/signatures-panel.png)
2. Under **My Signatures**, click **New signature…**.
3. Choose **Signature** or **Initials**, then how to make it:
   - **Draw** — sign with your mouse, pen or finger.
   - **Type** — type your name and choose a style (Script, Handwriting, Formal…).
   - **Image** — use a picture of your signature.

   ![Creating a typed signature](img/signature-create.png)
4. Pick the ink colour, give it a name if you like, and click **Save**.

## Sign a document

1. Optional: type a **Reason** (for example *Approved for construction*).
2. Click your signature in the panel.

   ![Your saved signature in the panel](img/signatures-mine.png)
3. Click on the page to place it, or drag a box to size it.

   ![The signature placed on the form](img/signature-placed.png)

## Who signed, and has it changed?

Under **In This Document**, the panel lists the signatures placed, who placed them and when, and warns if the document was changed after it was signed.

> **Note:** This is an *ink* signature: a picture of your signature. For a signature that proves who signed and that the file has not changed since, use a [Digital ID](#digital-sign).

Right-click a saved signature to **Rename…** or **Delete** it.
