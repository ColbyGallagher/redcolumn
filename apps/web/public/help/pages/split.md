# Split the view

Split the drawing area in two to look at two documents (or two parts of the same document) at once.

![Two drawings side by side in a split view](img/split-view.png)

## Split the view

1. Choose {{View > Split Vertical}} (or press [[Ctrl+2]]) for left and right panes.
   Choose {{View > Split Horizontal}} (or press [[Ctrl+H]]) for top and bottom panes.
2. Pick the PDF to show in the second pane and click **Open**.

Each pane has its own tabs, zoom and page. Click in a pane to make it the active one: menus and tools work on the active pane.

> **Tip:** You can also right-click a tab and choose **Open in Split View**.

## Move them together (Sync)

Turn on **Sync** in the status bar so both panes pan, zoom and change page together.

![The Sync button in the status bar](img/split-sync.png)

The {{View}} menu has two kinds of sync:

![View menu split commands](img/view-menu-split.png)

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Synchronise Document** | Both panes move together, and turn pages together. |
| **Synchronise Page** | Both panes pan and zoom together, but each stays on its own page. |
| **Switch** ([[Ctrl+1]]) | Swaps the two panes. |
| **Balance** ([[Shift+F12]]) | Makes both panes the same size. |
| **Unsplit** | Goes back to one pane. |

> **Tip:** Drag the bar between the panes to make one bigger.

> **Note:** To see the differences between two revisions highlighted for you, use [Compare](#compare) or [Overlay](#overlay) instead.
