# Bring to front, align and flip

Right-click a selected markup to find these.

![The markup right-click menu](img/markup-menu.png)

## Order (which is on top)

Right-click › **Order**:

![The Order menu](img/markup-menu-order.png)

| Command | Puts the markup… |
|---|---|
| **Bring to Front** | on top of all others |
| **Bring Forward** | up one level |
| **Send Backward** | down one level |
| **Send to Back** | under all others |

![Bringing the bottom rectangle to the front, then sending it to the back](gif/arrange.gif)

## Line up several markups

Select two or more markups, then right-click › **Alignment**:

![The Alignment menu](img/markup-menu-align.png)

![Three rectangles lined up along their left edges with Align Left](gif/align-left.gif)

![Three rectangles lined up along their middles with Align Middle](gif/align-middle.gif)

- **Align Left / Center / Right** — line up their sides or centres across.
- **Align Top / Middle / Bottom** — line them up down.
- **Distribute Horizontally / Vertically** — space three or more out evenly.
- **Center in Document** — move the selection, as it is laid out, to the middle of the page. Works on one markup or more.
- **Same Width / Same Height / Same Size** — resize the others to match the markup you selected first. Click that one first, then Shift-click the rest. Text boxes change shape but keep their font size.
- **Flip Horizontal / Vertical** — mirror the markups. Rotated boxes turn the other way too. Pictures and stamps move and turn, but their image is not mirrored.

These are also in the ribbon, in the Align group of the Modify tab.
