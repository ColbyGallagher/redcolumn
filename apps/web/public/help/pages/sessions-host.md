# Host a session

## Start a session

1. Open the PDFs you want to review.
2. Click **Sessions** on the panel rail, then **+ Start** (or right-click a tab › **Add to Session** › **Start New Session…**).
3. On the **General** tab:

   ![Start a New Session](img/session-start.png)
   - Type a **Session name**, for example *Level 2 coordination*.
   - Choose where to **Host the session in**: **My Google Drive** or **My OneDrive**. Sign in when asked.
   - Choose the **Options**: whether attendees who can comment may add documents, may save copies, and may invite others.
   - Optional: set when the session **Closes**.
4. Click **Next**. On the **Documents** tab, tick the PDFs to share.
5. Click **Next**. On the **Attendees** tab, add people by email and choose what each may do (see below).
6. Click **Start session**.

## Who can do what

| Level | Can |
|---|---|
| **View documents** | See the documents, markups and chat. |
| **Add comments, edit own** | Add markups, reply and set statuses; change only their own markups. |
| **Add comments, edit anyone's** | Change anyone's markups too. |
| **No access** | Nothing. |

Set a level for **Anyone else with the invite link**, for each person, and for **Groups** of people. To change it later, click **Permissions…** in the panel.

## Invite people

- Click **Invite**, type email addresses, and send. Their folder access is shared for you by Google Drive or OneDrive.
- Or click the link button to copy the invite link and send it any way you like.

## Manage the session

Click **Manage session…** to rename it, add or remove documents, change permissions, or end it.

- **Add documents:** **Add open** (open tabs) or **Add files…**.
- **Update a document to a new revision:** pick the new PDF. Markups stay on their pages.

## End the session

1. Click **End session…**.
2. Choose what to **Save before ending**: each document with the markups of the people you choose, the Record, every markup as CSV. Choose where to **Save to**: this computer, a new folder in your OneDrive, or a [Project](#projects).
3. Type the session's name to confirm, and end it.

The session folder and its files are removed for everyone. If anything fails to save, the session is not ended and nothing is removed.
