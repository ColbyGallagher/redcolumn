# Join a session

## From an invite link

1. Click the link the host sent. redcolumn opens and says **You're invited to a Live Session**.
2. Click **Join session**.
3. Sign in with your Microsoft account (personal, work or school) when asked.

## From the Sessions panel

1. Click **Sessions** on the panel rail, then **Join…**.

   ![Join a session](img/session-join.png)
2. Paste the session link the host sent.

## From a shared folder

If the host started the session in a shared folder (desktop app only):

1. Click **Sessions** on the panel rail, then **Join…**.
2. Click **Open the session folder on this computer…**, and pick the session's folder. It is named after the session, followed by *(redcolumn session)*.

You need to be able to open and change files in that folder. The app opens it again by itself the next time you start redcolumn.

## Before you start

Set your name: it is shown on every markup you make. Choose {{Edit}} and type it in **Author**, or set **User** in [Preferences](#preferences) › **General** › **Options**.

## Taking part

- Open the session's documents from the panel. Their tabs show **Live**.
- Mark up as normal. Your markups appear for everyone straight away, and theirs for you.
- Use **Chat** to talk, and right-click › **Send Markup Alert…** to point everyone at a markup.
- If you can only view, the panel says so: you can still read everything and chat.

Click **Leave session** when you are done. You can join again later from **My Sessions**.
