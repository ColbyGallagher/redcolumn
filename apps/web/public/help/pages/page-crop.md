# Crop pages

Cropping trims the edges of pages, for example to remove a large blank border or to keep only one detail.

1. Choose {{Document > Crop Pages…}} (or press [[Shift+Alt+O]]).

   ![The Crop Pages window](img/crop-pages.png)
2. Choose the **Pages** to crop.
3. Under **Crop to**, choose:
   - **Margins** — type how much to cut off the **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom** and **Left**, and pick the unit; **or**
   - **A rectangle drawn on the page** — click **Draw…**, then drag a box around the part to keep.
4. Click **OK**.

> **Note:** Cropping hides the trimmed area. Press [[Ctrl+Z]] to undo.
