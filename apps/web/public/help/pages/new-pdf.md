# Create a new PDF

## A blank PDF

1. Choose {{File > New PDF…}}.

   ![The New PDF window](img/new-pdf.png)
2. Leave **Start from** on **Blank pages**. Choose the **Page size** (Letter, A4, ARCH D…, or **Custom…** to type a width and height in inches or millimetres), **Portrait** or **Landscape**, and how many **Pages**.
3. Click **Create**.

The new PDF opens in a tab. Mark it up like any other document, and [save](#save) it.

## From a built-in template

{{File > New PDF…}} has ready-made sheets under **Start from**:

- **Drawing sheets** (A4 portrait; A3, A2, A1, A0, ANSI B, ANSI D and ARCH D landscape): a border with zone references, a revision table and a title block. The title block boxes (company, client, project, drawing title, drawn, checked, scale, drawing number, revision…) are form fields: click one and type.
- **Grid paper** (A4, A3 landscape): 5 mm squares with a heavier line every 10 mm.

Choose one, set how many **Pages**, and click **Create**. Each page gets its own title block.

## From your own template

Use any PDF you use often (your company's title block, a site report, a transmittal) as a template.

1. **Keep it as a template once**, any of these ways:
   - In {{File > New PDF…}}, click **Save Open PDF as Template** to keep the PDF that is open now.
   - In {{File > New PDF…}}, click **Import Template…** and pick a PDF on this computer.
   - In [File Access](#file-access), right-click the PDF and choose **Use as Template**.
2. **Make a new document from it:** in {{File > New PDF…}}, choose it under **Start from › My templates** and click **Create**. Or choose {{File > New PDF from Template…}}, pick the template, type a name, and click **Create**.

The new document is a copy, with the template's markups and form fields. The template itself is not changed. To stop offering a template, choose it and click **Remove Template** (the PDF stays in File Access).

**Tip:** start from a built-in drawing sheet, fill in your company and client, add your logo, then **Save Open PDF as Template**. Every new sheet then starts filled in.
