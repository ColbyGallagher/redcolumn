# Snapping

When snapping is on, the point you are placing jumps to a nearby corner, end or crossing, so your markups and measurements line up exactly with the drawing.

A small square shows where the point will snap:

![A snap marker on the corner of a wall](img/snap-marker.png)

## Turn snapping on and off

Use the switches in the status bar:

![The snap switches in the status bar](img/status-snap.png)

1. **Snap Grid** — to the dots of the [grid](#view-aids) ([[Ctrl+Shift+F9]]).
2. **Snap Content** — to lines, ends, midpoints and crossings in the PDF drawing ([[Ctrl+Shift+F8]]).
3. **Snap Markup** — to corners of your other markups ([[Ctrl+Shift+F7]]).

## Skip snapping for one point

Hold [[Alt]] while you click.

> **Tip:** Snapping to content needs a PDF made from CAD (vector lines). A scanned drawing has no lines to snap to.

Snapping when a document opens is set in [Preferences](#preferences) › **Grid & Snap**.
