# Magnifier and full screen

## Magnifier

The magnifier is a round lens that shows the area under the pointer two and a half times bigger, without changing your zoom.

1. Choose {{View > Magnifier}}.
2. Move the pointer over the drawing. The lens follows it.

   ![The magnifier over a light fitting](img/magnifier.png)
3. Choose {{View > Magnifier}} again to turn it off.

## Full screen

Full screen hides everything except the drawing, to give it the whole screen.

1. Choose {{View > Full Screen}} (or press [[F11]]).
2. Press [[Esc]] to leave full screen.
