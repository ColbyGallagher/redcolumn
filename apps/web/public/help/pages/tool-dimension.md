# Dimension

Draws a dimension line with arrows at both ends and the text you type, like a dimension on a drawing.

## Draw a dimension

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Lines & Shapes > Dimension}}.

   ![Tools menu with Dimension highlighted](img/pick-dimension.png)
2. Drag from one end of what you are dimensioning to the other.
3. Type the text to show, for example `26'-0"`, and click **OK**.

   ![The Dimension Text box](img/dimension-text.png)

   ![A dimension line across the kitchen](img/draw-dimension.png)

## Change the text

Double-click the dimension (with the Select tool) and type new text.

> **Warning:** A Dimension does **not** measure anything. The text is whatever you type. To measure a real distance from the drawing's scale, use [Length](#measure-length).
