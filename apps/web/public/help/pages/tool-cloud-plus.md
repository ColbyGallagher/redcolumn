# Cloud+ (cloud with a note)

Draws a cloud **and** a callout with your note in one go. This is the quickest way to mark a change and say what it is.

## Draw a Cloud+

1. Click **Cloud+** on the toolbar.

   ![The Cloud+ button on the toolbar](img/pick-cloudPlus.png)
2. Drag a cloud around the area, as for a [Cloud](#tool-cloud).
3. A callout appears next to the cloud with a box ready to type in. Type your note.

   ![Typing the note for a Cloud+](img/draw-cloud-plus-editing.png)
4. Press [[Ctrl+Enter]] or click somewhere else to finish.

   ![The finished cloud and its note](img/draw-cloud-plus.png)

The cloud and its note are **grouped**: they move together. Drag the note box on its own to move just the text; the arrow keeps pointing at the cloud.

> **Warning:** Pressing [[Esc]] while typing cancels the text you typed.
