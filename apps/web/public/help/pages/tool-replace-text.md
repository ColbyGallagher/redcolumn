# Replace Text

Strikes through words in the PDF and adds the words that should replace them, with a caret (‸) mark.

## Replace text

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Text Markup > Replace Text}}.

   ![Tools menu with Replace Text highlighted](img/pick-replaceText.png)
2. Drag across the words to replace.
3. Type the new wording and click **OK**.

   ![The Replace Text box](img/replace-text-ask.png)

The old words are struck through and a caret marks where the new words go.

   ![Struck-through text with a replacement caret](img/draw-replace-text.png)

To change the new wording, double-click the markup.
