# Move, resize and reshape

First [select](#select) the markup with the Select tool ([[V]]).

![A selected markup with handles and the round rotate handle](img/select-handles.png)

## Move

**Drag** the markup (not a handle) to a new place.

## Resize

Drag a **square handle** at a corner or side.

## Rotate

- Drag the **round handle** above the markup. Hold [[Shift]] to turn in 15° steps.
- Or right-click it and choose **Rotate** › **Rotate 90° Clockwise**, **Rotate 90° Counterclockwise**, **Rotate…** (type an angle) or **Reset Rotation**.

## Reshape a line, polyline or polygon

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Move a corner (vertex) | Drag its square handle. |
| Add a corner | Drag the small knob in the middle of a side. |
| Remove a corner | Hold [[Ctrl]] (or [[Alt]]) and click its handle. |
| Curve a side (measurements) | Hold [[Alt]] and drag the middle knob. |
| Round all the corners | Right-click › **Round All Corners**. |

## Move a callout's text

Drag the text box. The arrow keeps pointing at the same place. Drag the arrow tip handle to point somewhere else. Right-click › **Add Leader** adds another arrow.

> **Tip:** For exact sizes, turn on [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale). With a line, rectangle or polyline selected, its sizes show on the toolbar and you can type new ones.
