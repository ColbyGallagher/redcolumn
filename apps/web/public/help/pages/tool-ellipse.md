# Ellipse

Draws an oval or a circle.

## Draw an ellipse

1. Click **Ellipse** on the toolbar, or press [[E]].

   ![The Ellipse button on the toolbar](img/pick-ellipse.png)
2. Drag a box. The oval fills the box.

   ![An ellipse in the kitchen](img/draw-ellipse.png)

> **Tip:** Hold [[Shift]] while you drag to draw a perfect circle.

## Draw a circle from its centre

1. Turn on **Draw to Scale** in the status bar.
2. With the Ellipse tool picked, choose **Circle · radius** or **Circle · diameter** in the box that appears on the toolbar.
3. Click the centre, then type the size and press [[Enter]], or click a point on the edge.

See [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale).
