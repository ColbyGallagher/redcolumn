# Welcome to redcolumn

redcolumn is a free program for marking up and measuring construction drawings (PDFs). It runs in your web browser, or as an installed app, and keeps your files on your own computer.

## What you can do

<div class="cards">
<a href="#markup-basics"><b>Mark up drawings</b><span>Clouds, arrows, text, stamps, highlights and more.</span></a>
<a href="#measure-basics"><b>Measure and take off</b><span>Lengths, areas, counts and volumes, to scale.</span></a>
<a href="#markups-list"><b>Track every markup</b><span>A list you can filter, sort, give statuses and export.</span></a>
<a href="#compare"><b>Compare revisions</b><span>See exactly what changed between two drawings.</span></a>
<a href="#thumbnails"><b>Manage pages</b><span>Insert, delete, rotate, label and number pages.</span></a>
<a href="#sessions"><b>Review together</b><span>Live Sessions and shared Projects.</span></a>
</div>

## New here? Do these five things first

1. [Take a tour of the screen](#workspace) so you know what each area is called.
2. [Open a PDF](#open).
3. [Draw your first markup](#markup-basics).
4. [Set the scale](#scale) before you measure anything.
5. [Save your work](#save) back to a PDF.

## Finding your way around this help

- Use the **contents** on the left. Click a heading to open or close it.
- Type in **Search help** at the top left to find any page. Press [[/]] to jump to the search box.
- Click any screenshot to see it full size.
- Red numbered boxes in screenshots match the numbers in the text beside them.

> **Tip:** Press [[F1]] in redcolumn at any time to open this help.

> **Note:** Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them. redcolumn has no account and no server.
