# Layers

Layers let you show and hide groups of things on the page.

There are two kinds:

- **PDF layers** — layers that come in the PDF itself (for example *Electrical*, *Furniture*). You can show or hide them.
- **Markup layers** — your own layers for markups.

## Show or hide layers

1. Click **Layers** on the panel rail.

   ![The Layers panel](img/layers-panel.png)
2. Untick a layer to hide it. Tick it to show it again.

Under **Shown by the app**, **Hyperlinks** shows or hides the blue outlines on [sheet links](#links).

## Put markups on a layer

1. Select the markups.
2. Right-click › **Layer** › **New Layer…**, type a name, and press [[Enter]]. Or pick an existing layer.

   ![The Layer menu](img/markup-menu-layer.png)

To take markups off a layer, right-click › **Layer** › **No Layer**.

> **Note:** When you save, markup layers become real PDF layers, so other PDF programs can show and hide them too.
