# Elliptical Arc

Draws part of an oval (ellipse). Use it for curved walls, swings and other curves that are not part of a circle.

## Draw an elliptical arc

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Lines & Shapes > Elliptical Arc}}.

   ![Tools menu with Elliptical Arc highlighted](img/pick-ellipticalArc.png)
2. Drag a box. The oval that fits in that box is used, and part of it is drawn.

   ![An elliptical arc](img/draw-elliptical-arc.png)

## Choose how much of the oval is drawn

1. Select the arc (press [[V]] and click it).
2. Open the [Properties panel](#properties).
3. Under **Arc**, change **Start** and **End**. They are angles in degrees, measured counter-clockwise from the right (east).

Drag the corner handles to change the size of the oval.
