# Volume

Measures a volume from an area and a depth: concrete slabs, excavation, fill.

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Volume}} (or press [[Alt+Shift+V]]).

   ![Tools, Measure, Volume](img/pick-volume.png)
2. Click around the outline, and click the first corner to close it (as for [Area](#measure-area)).

   ![A volume outline over the kitchen](img/measure-volume.png)
3. Select the volume and open the [Properties panel](#properties).
4. Type the **Depth**, for example `6"` or `0.15` (in the page's units), and press [[Enter]].

   ![The Depth box in the Properties panel](img/properties-volume.png)

The volume (for example in cubic feet, *cf*, or cubic metres) is shown on the page and in the list.

> **Note:** A new volume shows **0** until you enter a depth.

> **Tip:** Any [Area](#measure-area) becomes a volume when you give it a depth, and [Smart Fill](#smart-fill) can make volumes directly.
