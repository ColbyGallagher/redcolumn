# Diameter and Radius

Measure the size of a circle: columns, pipes, tanks, round rooms.

## Diameter

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Diameter}}.

   ![Tools, Measure, Diameter](img/pick-diameter.png)
2. Click one side of the circle, then the opposite side.

   ![A diameter measurement](img/measure-diameter.png)

## Radius

1. Choose {{Tools > Measure > Radius}}.

   ![Tools, Measure, Radius](img/pick-radius.png)
2. Click the **centre** of the circle, then a point on its edge.

   ![A radius measurement](img/measure-radius.png)

Both draw the circle they measure, so you can check it lines up with the drawing.
