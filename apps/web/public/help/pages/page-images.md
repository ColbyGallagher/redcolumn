# Save pages as images

Save pages as picture files (PNG, JPEG or TIFF), for reports, presentations, websites or document systems.

1. Choose {{File > Export > Page Images…}}.

   ![The Export Page Images window](img/page-images.png)
2. Choose the **Format**:
   - **PNG (sharp lines)** — best for drawings.
   - **JPEG** — smaller files, best for photos and scans.
   - **TIFF** — for document and archive systems. Its compression, and whether all the pages go into one multi-page file, are set in [Preferences](#preferences) › Import/Export › Images; CCITT Group 4 (the default) is black and white.
3. Choose the **Colour** (as drawn, colour, greyscale or black and white) and the **Resolution**. Higher is sharper but makes bigger files. Both start from Preferences › Import/Export › Images.
4. Choose the **Pages**: **Current**, **All**, or a list like `1-3`.
5. Untick **Include markups** to save the drawing without your markups.
6. Click **Export**.

Several pages are saved together in a `.zip` file. Pages without a sheet number are named by the **Page Number** pattern in Preferences (`Page 001` by default).

> **Tip:** For just part of a page, use [Snapshot](#tool-snapshot).
