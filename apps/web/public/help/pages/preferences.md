# Preferences

Preferences change how redcolumn behaves on this device. Changes apply straight away.

Open them with {{File > Preferences…}} (or press [[Ctrl+K]]). Click a section on the left. Click **Done** to close, or **Reset to Defaults** to undo every change.

## General

![Preferences: General](img/prefs-general.png)

- **Author name** — the name on every markup you make. (Also in the {{Edit}} menu's **Author** box.)
- **Units** — **Imperial** (feet, inches) or **Metric**. Scale and unit lists only offer this system.
- **Confirm before deleting markups** — asks before [[Delete]] removes markups.

## Interface

![Preferences: Interface](img/prefs-interface.png)

- **Open Recent** — how many documents {{File > Open Recent}} lists, and how far back.
- **Reopen the documents that were open** — the tabs you had open come back when redcolumn starts.

## Navigation & Zoom

![Preferences: Navigation & Zoom](img/prefs-navigation-zoom.png)

- **Ctrl + wheel zoom step** and **Wheel zoom step** — how far each notch of the mouse wheel zooms.
- **Invert wheel zoom** — wheel up zooms out.
- **Zoom in / out step** — how far the **+** and **−** buttons and keys zoom.

## Display

![Preferences: Display](img/prefs-display.png)

- **Page colours** — **As printed**, **Dark Mode**, or **Dimmer**. See [Dark pages](#display).
- **Disable line weights**, **Rulers**, **Full-screen crosshair**.
- **Always show reply indicators**.
- **Markups the list's filter leaves out** — show, dim or hide them on the page.
- **Show Spaces** on the page.

## Grid & Snap

![Preferences: Grid & Snap](img/prefs-grid-snap.png)

- **Show grid** and **Grid spacing** (on the paper).
- **Snap to grid**, **Snap to content**, **Snap to markup**. See [Snapping](#snapping).

## Markup

![Preferences: Markup](img/prefs-markup.png)

- **Snap to drawing geometry** — whether snapping to the drawing is on when a document opens.
- **Drag on the page to select markups** — with Select, dragging on an empty part of the page draws a selection box instead of panning.
- **Show hyperlink areas** — outlines [sheet links](#links).

## Tools

![Preferences: Tools](img/prefs-tools.png)

- **Reuse tools** — see [Reuse a tool](#reuse).
- **Draw to Scale** — see [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale).
- **Eraser** size, and **Erase whole markups**. See [Eraser](#tool-eraser).

## Tool Library

How clicking a saved tool works. See [Use the Tool Library](#tool-library).

## Spelling

Words you added with **Add to Dictionary**. Remove any you added by mistake.

## Snapshot

**Resolution** of the pictures [Snapshot](#tool-snapshot) copies.

## Offline

![Preferences: Offline](img/prefs-offline.png)

- Whether the app is saved for offline use on this device.
- **Storage** — how much space your documents use, and **Keep on This Device**, which asks the browser not to clear your documents when space runs low.
- **OCR languages** — download them now so [OCR](#ocr) works offline.

> **Tip:** Settings for *what the screen shows* (toolbar, panels, tool library, list columns) are kept in [Profiles](#profiles) instead, so you can switch them all at once.
