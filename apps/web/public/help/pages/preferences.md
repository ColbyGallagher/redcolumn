# Preferences

Preferences change how redcolumn behaves on this device.

Open them with {{File > Preferences…}} (or press [[Ctrl+K]]). Pick a category on the left, then a tab along the top. Type in **Search** to find a setting: only the categories and tabs that have it are left, and the matching words are highlighted.

Click **OK** to keep your changes, or **Cancel** to forget them. **Defaults** puts every setting back as it came (press **OK** to keep that too). **Export** saves your preferences, name and custom words to a file; **Import** reads such a file back, for example on another computer.

Settings marked with a small circle (○) are saved, imported and exported with the rest, but redcolumn does not use them yet.

## General

![Preferences: General › Options](img/prefs-general-options.png)

- **Options** — **User** (the name on every markup you make; also the **Author** box in the {{Edit}} menu), **Tab Truncation** (which part of a long document name its tab cuts short), **Show Full-Screen Crosshair**, and **Reopen Files from Last Session on Startup**.
- **Document** — **Maximum Zoom**, the furthest you can zoom in.
- **Navigation** — what the plain mouse wheel does in single page and in continuous view (**Zoom** or **Scroll**), **Reverse Mouse Wheel when Zooming**, and under **Zoom Steps** how far each wheel notch, [[Ctrl]]+wheel notch and **+** / **−** press zooms.
- **Grid & Snap** — the grid's **Units**, **Grid Spacing** (on the paper), **Show Grid** and **Snap to Grid**; **Snap to Content** and **Snap to Markup**, which kinds of points snap (**Lines**, **Mid-Points**, **End-Points**, **Intersections**), the snap **Sensitivity** in screen pixels and the **Snap Colour**. See [Snapping](#snapping).
- **Spelling** — **Enable Spell Check** underlines misspelt words as you type markup text and comments. **Include Upper Case Words** makes [Check Spelling](#spelling) check words in capitals too (off, it skips them: on drawings they are mostly abbreviations). **Custom Words** are the words you added: **+** adds one, **×** removes the selected one.

![Preferences: General › Grid & Snap](img/prefs-general-grid-snap.png)

## Interface

- **File Access** — how many documents {{File > Open Recent}} lists, and how far back.
- **Markups List** — **Zoom Fit Markups when selected** (off, clicking a markup in the list only goes to its page), what the page does with **Filtered Markups** (show, dim or hide them), and how faint dimmed ones are.
- **Display** — **Page Colours** (**As printed**, **Dark Mode** or **Dimmer**; see [Dark pages](#display)), **Disable Line Weights**, **Show Rulers**, **Show Spaces** and **Show Hyperlink Areas** (outlines [sheet links](#links)).

![Preferences: Interface › Markups List](img/prefs-interface-markups-list.png)

## Tools

![Preferences: Tools › Markup](img/prefs-tools-markup.png)

- **Markup** — **Reuse Tools** (see [Reuse a tool](#reuse)), **Always Show Reply Indicators**, **Snapshot Resolution** (of the pictures [Snapshot](#tool-snapshot) copies), **Confirm before Deleting Markups**, and **Drag on the Page to Select Markups** (with Select, dragging on an empty part of the page draws a selection box instead of panning).
- **Measure** — **Measurement System**: **Imperial** (feet, inches) or **Metric**. Scale and unit lists only offer this system.
- **Sketch** — **Ellipse Mode** and **Draw to Scale**. See [Draw to Scale](#draw-to-scale).
- **Eraser** — its size, and **Erase Whole Markups**. See [Eraser](#tool-eraser).
- **Tool Library** — how clicking a saved tool works. See [Use the Tool Library](#tool-library).

## Sets

Options, sorting, categories and tags for drawing [Sets](#sets). **Categories** lists each template's categories and the start of the sheet number that puts a sheet in each: **+** and **×** add and remove rows, **New** starts an empty template, **Save As…** copies the template under a new name, and **Save** and **Load…** write a template to a file and read it back.

![Preferences: Sets › Categories](img/prefs-sets-categories.png)

## Import/Export

Settings for converting to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and for photos and image files.

## Advanced

![Preferences: Advanced › Offline](img/prefs-advanced-offline.png)

**Offline**:

- Whether the app is saved for offline use on this device.
- **Storage** — how much space your documents use, and **Keep on This Device**, which asks the browser not to clear your documents when space runs low.
- **OCR languages** — download them now so [OCR](#ocr) works offline.

> **Tip:** Settings for *what the screen shows* (toolbar, panels, tool library, list columns) are kept in [Profiles](#profiles) instead, so you can switch them all at once.
