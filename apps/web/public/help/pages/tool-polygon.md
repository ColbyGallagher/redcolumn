# Polygon

Draws a closed shape with any number of straight sides. Use it to outline rooms and areas that are not rectangles.

## Draw a polygon

1. Choose {{Tools > Markup > Lines & Shapes > Polygon}}, or press [[Shift+P]].

   ![Tools menu with Polygon highlighted](img/pick-polygon.png)
2. Click each corner in turn.

   ![A polygon being drawn around the kitchen](img/draw-polygon-progress.png)
3. To finish, click the **first** corner again, double-click, or press [[Enter]]. The shape closes itself.

   ![The finished polygon](img/draw-polygon.png)

> **Tip:** Press [[Backspace]] to remove the last corner you clicked.

## Change it

Select it (press [[V]] and click it). Drag a corner handle to move it. Drag the small knob in the middle of a side to add a corner. Hold [[Ctrl]] and click a corner to remove it.

> **Note:** To measure the area inside, use [Area](#measure-area).
