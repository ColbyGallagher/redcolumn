# Undo, redo and Undo History

## Undo or redo one step

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Undo the last change | Press [[Ctrl+Z]], or click **↶** on the toolbar. |
| Redo it | Press [[Ctrl+Y]] (or [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]]), or click **↷** on the toolbar. |

You can undo again and again, right back to when you opened the document.

Undo covers every change you make to a document, not just markups:

- **Pages:** deleting, inserting, moving, rotating, replacing and cropping pages, Page Setup, and Slip Sheet.
- **The file's contents:** Flatten and Unflatten, Redact and Erase Content, Edit Text, OCR, headers and footers, Document Properties, Reduce File Size, Colour Processing and Repair.
- **Everything attached to the pages:** markups, scales, viewports, sheet numbers, links, bookmarks, Places and custom columns.

Undoing a page change puts the pages back as they were, along with any markups that were on them.

> **Note:** A Live Session document is shared with everyone in the session, so changes to its pages and file cannot be undone here.

## Undo many steps at once

1. Click {{Edit > Undo History…}}.

   ![The Undo History window](img/undo-history.png)
2. Click a step. That step **and every step after it** are undone.
3. Click **Close**.

> **Tip:** Undid too much? Press [[Ctrl+Y]] to redo, one step at a time.

> **Note:** Each document has its own undo history. Undo only affects the document in the tab you are looking at.
